About Us

Cinema as a tool for truth

Awani Productions Limited is a Nigerian production company based in Lagos. Founded on 14 September 2024, we exist to redefine and uplift African narratives through cinema — telling compelling stories that engage audiences locally and internationally.

Our vision is to place African cinema on the global stage, not as a novelty, but as a legitimate and powerful force in world culture.

Leadership
KA

Khalil Agboola

Founder & CEO

Born and raised in Rivers State, Nigeria, Khalil is a Lagos-based filmmaker and engineer. Formally trained as an Electrical/Electronic Engineer at Cardiff University, Wales, he chose to pursue a career in film after spending his university years seeking solace and inspiration from cinema.

A multidisciplinary filmmaker versed in production, directing, screenwriting, and montage, Khalil has had his work screened across Nigeria to widespread acclaim. He founded Awani Productions in 2024 with the singular aim of redefining African narratives through film.
